Title: Badge

----

Excerpt: Zero-JS, CSS-only badge primitive. Five variants, icon and spinner support. Renders as <span> or <a>.

----

Intro: <p>A compact inline label element for status indicators, category tags, and counts. No JavaScript. Include anywhere in a Twig template with a single <code>{% include %}</code> call. Renders as <code>&lt;span&gt;</code> by default; add <code>href</code> to switch to <code>&lt;a&gt;</code>.</p>
